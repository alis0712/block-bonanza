# Everyone Alive, used only as a shape

Local H's "Everyone Alive" is on *Whatever Happened to P.J. Soles?* (2004). The album track is 3 minutes 56 seconds. Scott Lucas and Brian St. Clair wrote it, and the Burnout 3 soundtrack used it. A published tempo for this song was not found, so the beds here do not aim at a number taken from the record.

Local H is a two-piece. Lucas plays a guitar rig that sends the low strings to a bass amp, so one fuzzy instrument covers guitar and bass. St. Clair plays drums. Notes on the Burnout version call out the drumming. The sound people describe is a thick, low, distorted guitar and a kit, getting louder as the song stacks up, not a pad and not a drum machine.

These beds copy none of that recording. Not the riff, not a melody, not a lyric. What they copy is the shape of a song that long.

The title and the menu are still that one locked phrase. From v53, each stage is a different full-band performance, so the guitar and the drums were already played together. The loud chorus does not fall at the same time on every stage.
